      How Smart Bracket works

      What it does

      Smart Bracket reads your recent games in the current mode and sets two numbers: a floor, the rating where EloGuard steps in and ends your session, and a target to aim for. The bracket refreshes when a guarded session starts, then the target stays fixed for that session.

      Your floor

      The floor protects your rating. If you've dropped several games in a row, it's pulled up close to stop the bleed early. Otherwise it sits far enough below you to allow a normal run of losses, based on how swingy your recent games are.

      Your target

      Your target is for this session only. It follows your recent performance, normal winning runs, and current trend. Hit it and EloGuard banks the win. You may deliberately extend the target once after a short pause; the floor tightens at the same time.

      Long-term goal

      Your Elo goal is shown below the session bracket for context, but it never raises or lowers the current session's floor or target.

      Trailing stop-loss

      When on, your floor rises as your rating peaks during a session, so gains get locked in instead of given back. A floor hit is always final for the session and never offers a continue option.

      Fallback range

      If there isn't enough recent game data to compute a bracket, the floor and target are simply set this many points below and above your current rating.

      Every number is explained

      Each applied bracket comes with plain-English reasons on the home screen, telling you exactly why the floor and target landed where they did.
